MISSION BRIEFING

1. PILOTING: The ship follows your mouse cursor with organic drag.

2. PULSE (L-CLICK): Clear small enemies. 6s Cooldown.

3. SUPERNOVA (R-CLICK): Wipe the screen, including Bosses. 25s Cooldown.

4. ESCAPE: Use the ESC key to pause the simulation.

5. SURVIVAL: Do not touch the red Sentrons. Good luck.

6. ⚡ SURVIVAL MODE: Side-scrolling blitz. Move with A/D or ←/→, shoot with SPACE. Kill bosses for coins. Collect power-ups and re-use them with coins. Keys 1–4 activate your stored power-ups.
